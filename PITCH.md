# Miniature Earth — 90-second pitch

**0–12 seconds — A world small enough to care about**
“Deep time is hard to feel. Extinction can seem like a distant statistic. Miniature Earth puts a living world on your desk.” Open the island and start Watch demo.

**12–28 seconds — Build a balance**
“Place ferns, grazers and a friendly predator. These are simple ecological rules: plants grow, animals need food, and a suitable home matters.” Point to the Jurassic garden and its population panel.

**28–40 seconds — Time changes everything**
“Slide through the asteroid. The dinosaurs become fossils, while hardy ferns return. The toy makes a real pattern in Earth's history visible.”

**40–52 seconds — Remember a life**
“Meet the dodo before its disappearance. Some losses are permanent.” Let the memorial appear. “Our story cards cite their sources and leave uncertain numbers blank.”

**52–72 seconds — Same world, different choices**
“Now add today's creatures, including tiger, red panda, giant panda and hawksbill. Move to 2060. Protect habitats, then switch to Exploit. The same placements face different pressures.” Point to the outcome panel. “This is a game simulation, not a forecast.”

**72–90 seconds — What comes next**
“We built 53 toy organisms, three eras of islands, a deterministic simulation and 36 conservation stories in a browser. Next: fill the remaining verified histories, deepen habitat and food-web interactions, and refine accessibility and performance on more phones. Our aim is simple: make people curious enough to care.”
