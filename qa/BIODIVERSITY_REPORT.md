# Miniature Earth biodiversity expansion report

Implementation and validation were completed locally. Nothing was pushed or deployed. The default evidence region remains North America. Modelled counts are illustrative representatives, not historical censuses.

Those are labeled **CONTENT MISSING**, never as evidence that Earth was lifeless. The other 45 sampled dates contain eligible organisms, although 33 are still labeled **PARTIALLY POPULATED**. ## Required final report

1. **Previously empty ranges:** 30/47 sampled dates were empty before this pass. Twenty-eight now contain default organisms. The remaining gaps are 600 and 580 million years ago in the North American region. New sampled dates sit between the 24 requested checkpoints; this is not proof of continuous coverage between samples.
2. **Root causes:** overly narrow fossil snapshots and regional windows; missing group-level records and playable models; founding groups placed without habitat-aware anchors; plants hidden behind the modern vegetation shortcut; incorrect habitat focus; and focus applied before asynchronous models finished loading. Date-unit boundary behavior also needed explicit testing.
3. **New playable organisms:** Cloudina tube colony, Placerias, Dilophosaurus, Camarasaurus, Deinonychus, Diplomystus lake predator, Ribbed brachiopod, Cup crinoid, Eurypterus, Cooksonia-like early plant, Archaeopteris tree, Dunkleosteus, Knightia lake fish, Fossil dawn redwood, Three-toed browsing horse, Three-toed grazing horse, Coelophysis, Edmontonia. These are 18 original genus or higher-group reconstructions with source-linked taxonomy, dated occurrence windows, habitat data, stylized models, icons, LODs, and scene integration. This does not make each genus a precise species occurrence.
4. **Plants and vegetation:** added Cooksonia-like early vascular plants, Archaeopteris trees, and fossil Metasequoia; expanded screened regional coverage for ferns, conifers, cycads, oaks, grasses, lycophytes, and horsetails. Forests, understory and wetland plants use separate clusters. Flowering plants remain excluded from pre-Cretaceous periods.
5. **Playable organisms:** 90 in `species.json` (up from 72 before this expansion).
6. **Taxonomy-reference-only records:** 239. They remain separate from playable organisms.
7. **Periods with visible life:** sampled scenes cover late Ediacaran, Cambrian, Ordovician, Silurian, Devonian, Carboniferous, Permian, Triassic, Jurassic, Cretaceous, Paleogene, Neogene, Pleistocene, Holocene, modern and future settings. Some dates show plant-only or one-group communities; these are marked partial.
8. **Scientifically sparse periods:** early Ediacaran macrofossil evidence is regionally uneven; soft tissue, feeding and genus-level timing are uncertain. A narrow North American occurrence interval is not generalized to the whole period.
9. **Content still missing:** North American 600 and 580 Ma in this catalogue, plus unsampled dates and under-researched region/period combinations. The catalogue does not yet provide a complete local food web for every scene. “Limited fossil evidence” is not inferred from missing game records.
10. **Automatic habitat focus:** the game ranks eligible, asset-backed communities, weighting animal groups more than plants, then eases the camera to the most informative land, freshwater or marine habitat on assemblage changes. Auto focus is optional; habitat controls remain manual, and camera framing never changes the evidence region. Nearby-date suggestions are explicit.
11. **Marine visibility:** yes. Mobile marine representatives and benthic organisms are visible through the marine inspection view; attachments use the seafloor and water opacity is reduced for inspection. This is a stylized diorama, not physical underwater optics.
12. **Spawning improvements:** regional time-window validation; habitat-specific deterministic anchors; visible historic plant batches; distinct forest/wetland/coast profiles; per-date eligible, asset-backed, spawned and loaded counts. Historical auto-population stays separate from intentionally empty custom islands.
13. **Transitions:** stable compatible cohort identities and anchors, cached history, deterministic rewind, eased camera focus, and follow-up focus after async model load. Scrubbing adjacent dates does not respawn a compatible cohort at every movement.
14. **Scientific limitations:** PBDB fossil deposit intervals are uncertain display windows, not exact lifespans or continuous occupation. Bounded US/Canada queries are not comprehensive occurrence inventories. Global abundance, local co-occurrence, many exact dates, tolerances, diets and future outcomes remain unknown or explicitly illustrative. The 239 reference entries are not finished simulations. See source links and uncertainty notes in `src/sim/data/metadata.json` and raw records in `qa/evidence/biodiversity/`.
15. **Performance:** 240 repeated habitat-anchor lookups improved from 40.22 ms to 0.88 ms median in the isolated benchmark (45.6× for that workload). The separate 22-group, 1280×800 SwiftShader scene measured 66.7 ms median / 100.0 ms p95, 38 draw calls and 195,196 triangles. This software-renderer result is not a native GPU/mobile benchmark or 60-FPS claim. Dense scenes still need hardware profiling and further optimization.
16. **Automated checks:** PASS: strict typecheck, production build, 120 unit tests / 24 files, 93 base GLB contracts, 90 LOD contracts, 70 rig checks (normalized weights, nine clips and deformed bounds), and Khronos validation for 253 GLBs with zero errors or warnings.
17. **Browser and visual checks:** PASS: 47/47 requested and midpoint visits, zero browser errors, 47 deterministic rewinds and 47 current screenshots. PASS: 18/18 reviewed organism models load and frame; animation rigs and motion checks passed where applicable. PASS: mobile notice overlap, pointer interaction and horizontal overflow checks; zero page errors. The screenshots and icon sheet were opened and reviewed.
18. **Files changed:** history eligibility, coverage focus, scene placement/rendering, species metadata and records, original model assets from the preceding locally committed expansion steps, browser audit results/screenshots, and this report. `src/scene/RiggedOrganism.tsx` now defers cleanup across React Strict Mode's development remount, preventing Three.js `AnimationMixer` cache errors; coverage notices now accept pointer input above the canvas. The user's `.gitignore` edit is excluded.
19. **Commit:** recorded in the final local milestone commit after acceptance; no push or deployment.
20. **Remaining work:** broaden source-reviewed coverage beyond North America, add a richer set of Carboniferous amphibians and further small/underrepresented groups when exact support and models are available, fill the two early Ediacaran regional gaps only with evidence, deepen local food webs, and profile dense scenes on physical GPU/mobile hardware. No unsupported taxa were inserted to hide these gaps.
